# Ponoko Pricing Model (Prototype)

> This is the published formula behind every Ponoko instant quote.
> Feed it real part specs and you land within a few cents of the
> number our system returns.

## How We Calculate a Quote

Estimated Cost Per Part =
    Type Base Rate x Complexity Multiplier x Size Multiplier x Quantity Multiplier

## Type Base Rate (at Moderate / Medium / Qty 100)

  Sheet Metal Fabrication .......... $2.36
  Laser Cutting & Engraving ........ $2.01
  Photochemical Machining .......... $2.71
  3D Printing ....................... $3.66
  Injection Molding (ex. tooling) ... $0.59
  Assembled PCBs ..................... $5.66

## Complexity Multiplier

  Simple ... 0.72      Moderate ... 1.00      Heavy ... 1.48

## Part Size Multiplier

  Small .... 0.68      Medium ... 1.00      Large ... 1.75

## Quantity Multiplier

  1 ... 2.30   10 ... 1.55   100 ... 1.00
  500 ... 0.76   1,000 ... 0.58   10,000 ... 0.37

## Every Quote Is a Starting Point

Ponoko matches any competitor's price on the same spec. Startups get
flexible terms, twelve-month payment plans, and the option to pay in
equity instead of cash on qualifying orders.
